## Full description (max 4000 characters)

```
GaGa Chat is a fast, private messenger for Android. Message friends and family, make crystal-clear HD voice and video calls, share photos, files and live location, plan your day and organise shared tasks — all in one app. Free over Wi-Fi or mobile data.

WHY GAGA CHAT
• Free, private and worldwide — no per-message charges.
• Built for real life: chatting, calling, sharing and planning in a single place.
• Designed to stay fast and reconnect automatically, even on weak networks.

MESSAGING
• Instant messages with delivered and read receipts and typing indicators.
• Reply, react, forward or delete any message with a long press.
• Group chats that keep families, classes and teams together.
• Instant search across your whole chat history.
• Message requests keep unknown senders out of your inbox.

HD VOICE & VIDEO CALLS
• Crystal-clear voice and video calls over Wi-Fi or mobile data.
• A clean, focused call screen with mute, speaker, camera and flip controls.
• Reconnects automatically when your connection drops.

MEDIA & FILES
• Send full-resolution photos and videos, and build multi-photo albums.
• Review and annotate photos before you send them.
• Share PDFs and documents up to 50 MB and open them with a compatible app.
• Voice messages when typing is not convenient.

LIVE LOCATION
• Share where you are with an accuracy and freshness preview.
• You choose exactly when to send — nothing is shared automatically.

DAILY LIFE
• Reminders without leaving your chats.
• Split bills with friends, roommates or trip groups in a few taps.
• Shared shopping lists so nothing gets forgotten.
• Safety check-in to let people you trust know you are safe.
• GaGa Today: a single dashboard for tasks, reminders and what needs your attention.

PRIVACY & SAFETY
• Add friends by QR code and keep control of who can reach you.
• Privacy controls for online visibility, friend lists and searchability.
• Blocked-users list, anti-spam protections and an app lock.
• Hidden chats and active-session management.

MADE FOR YOUR LANGUAGE
Use the app in English, বাংলা or 中文, and translate individual messages in 15+ languages.

COMING SOON
• GaGa Wallet (coins) — a wallet experience is being prepared; no live custody, transfers or withdrawals are offered yet.
• Business, Premium and Delivery tools are planned and clearly marked as coming soon in the app.

GaGa Chat keeps things simple: one app for the conversations and the everyday tasks that go with them. Download it and say hello.

Support: support@gagachat.app
Privacy policy: https://gagachat.app/privacy.html
Terms: https://gagachat.app/terms.html
```
