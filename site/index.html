Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is GaGa Chat free?

Yes. Messaging, voice calls, and video calls are free. Optional premium features and Gaga Coins are available.

Is there a web version?

GaGa Chat is distributed as a native Android app. This website is an information page only — the full experience lives in the app.

Do I need a VPN?

No. GaGa Chat is designed to work globally without a VPN.

Which devices are supported?

Any Android device running Android 5.1 (API 22) or newer.

How do I get support?

Email support@gagachat.app or use the Help Center inside the app.

Is my data private?

Yes. We use modern authentication and encryption, and we never sell your personal data. See our Privacy Policy.
