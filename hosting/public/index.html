Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is GaGa Chat really free?
Yes. Messaging, HD voice and video calls, and photo, video, document and location sharing are free over Wi-Fi or mobile data. There are no ads.
Which devices are supported?
GaGa Chat runs on Android 8.0 (Oreo) and newer, on both 64-bit and 32-bit ARM devices.
Are my messages private?
Your media is stored privately and delivered over encrypted TLS connections. Private chat media is only shared with the people you send it to, and you can delete your account at any time.
How do I install the app?
Download the APK from the download page, open it, allow installation when prompted, verify your phone number and start chatting.
Can I use GaGa Chat across countries?
Yes. GaGa Chat connects people across borders and time zones, with support for 40+ languages.
