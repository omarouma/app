FAQ

Questions, answered

Is GaGa Chat free?

Yes. Messaging and voice and video calls are free over Wi-Fi or mobile data. Your carrier may charge for data.

Which devices are supported?

GaGa Chat is available for Android today. Download the APK from this site.

Is the wallet real money?

Wallet and coin features are planned for a later release. Availability and terms will be announced when ready.

How is my data protected?

Connections are encrypted with TLS, media is stored privately, and you can block, report, and delete your account. Read our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I get help?

Email support@gagachat.app and we will get back to you.
