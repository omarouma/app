Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information GaGa Chat collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. We designed GaGa Chat to collect as little as possible while keeping your conversations working reliably for users around the world.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details — the email address, phone number and display name you provide when you register.
  • Messages and media — the text, photos, videos, voice notes, files and locations you choose to send.
  • Contacts — the phone contacts you explicitly share to find friends.
  • Device and usage data — app version, device model and crash diagnostics used to keep the app stable.

2. How we use information

We use your information to deliver messages and calls, show your conversations, provide notifications, keep your account secure, and improve the Service. We do not sell your personal information.

3. Storage and security

Messages and media are stored on secure cloud infrastructure. Private media is stored in access-controlled buckets and served to recipients through short-lived signed links. Your session credentials are encrypted on your device.

4. Sharing

We share information only with the service providers needed to run GaGa Chat (for example, cloud hosting and real-time call infrastructure), or where required by law. We never share your messages with advertisers.

5. Your choices

You can control read receipts, mute or archive chats, delete messages, and delete your account from Settings. Deleting your account removes your profile and associated data from our systems, subject to legal retention requirements.

6. Children

GaGa Chat is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. We will notify you of material changes in the app. Continued use after changes take effect constitutes acceptance.

8. Contact

Questions about privacy? Contact us at privacy@gagachat.app.
